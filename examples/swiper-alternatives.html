Swiper alternatives for vanilla JS, compared

Swiper is the safe default and still the most complete carousel on npm. But most sliders only need slides, controls and sane accessibility — and that is where a smaller engine earns its keep. Here is how the main alternatives differ.

Updated 2026-11-229 min readLive demo

What actually matters

Bundle costWhat you ship on every page load, including the CSS and the modules you enable.
Accessibility defaultsWhether roles, labels, live regions and focus isolation work without extra code.
LifecycleWhat happens on teardown in a SPA, and whether your original markup returns.

Swiper wins on breadth: virtual slides, dozens of effects and modules, and official wrappers for React, Vue and Web Components. If you need those, stop here and use Swiper. The comparison below is for sliders where that surface is more than you want to maintain.

How to read it

  • Embla and Keen are smaller because they are primitives. You get dragging and snapping, then you build arrows, bullets and accessible markup yourself. That is a feature, not a flaw — if you already have a design system, it is the cleanest path.
  • Splide is the closest accessibility peer. Its WAI-ARIA carousel pattern is mature and documented; it does not isolate off-screen slides with inert.
  • Swiper's a11y module is excellent but opt-in. If a teammate forgets the module or an import, the carousel silently loses announcements and focus isolation.
  • Zikra sits in the middle. It is bigger than Embla or Keen because it includes controls, seven layouts, accessibility behavior and the animation ladder — but smaller than Swiper core plus the modules most projects enable.

When to stay with Swiper

Choose Swiper if you need virtual slides for thousands of items, one of its extra effects (cube, flip, creative), Angular/Svelte wrappers, or a large ecosystem of plugins and Stack Overflow answers. Migration is only worth it when your slider is a small part of the page and you want the engine to be a small part of your bundle.

Frequently asked questions

Is Zikra Slider Pro a drop-in replacement for Swiper?

No. The APIs are different, so a Swiper configuration has to be mapped option by option and re-tested. Simple sliders move quickly, but it is not a find-and-replace.

Which is the smallest Swiper alternative?

By raw bundle size, Keen Slider (5.9 kB gzipped) and Embla (6.9 kB) are smaller because they ship fewer features and leave controls and accessibility markup to you. Zikra is 16.6 kB gzipped including seven layouts, controls, accessibility defaults and the animation ladder.

Does Zikra support the effects I use in Swiper?

It ships slide, fade, scale, coverflow, stack, cards and a custom transform layout. Swiper also has cube, flip and creative effects, which Zikra does not implement.

Can I migrate a Swiper slider without rewriting the markup?

Usually the markup carries over. Zikra works on direct slide children and snapshots your classes, ids and inline styles on init. The JavaScript API differs, so options and event handlers need to be mapped and leftover Swiper classes and wrappers should be removed first.
